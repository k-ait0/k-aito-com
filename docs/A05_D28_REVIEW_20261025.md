## Release decisions

- Do not silently edit the existing versioned question bank. Revise under a new question-set version and hash.
- Keep E as an auxiliary preference, not an objective measure of finances, time scarcity or wellbeing.
- Do not infer psychological reliability from automated scoring tests.
- Obtain content review and user interviews before public launch.
- Do not collect answers or behavioral analytics without explicit consent.
